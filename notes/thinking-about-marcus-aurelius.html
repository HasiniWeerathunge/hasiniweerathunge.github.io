I've Been Thinking About a Quote by Marcus Aurelius

Building a Life HW · 06.28.2026 · Entry 1

"Think of yourself as dead. You have lived your life. Now take what's left and live properly."

I don't interpret it as being about death, but about chapters.

Looking back, my life has unfolded through distinct stages—school, university, work, grad school, and now, postdoc. Each chapter ended with a different version of myself stepping into the next one, carrying forward the lessons but leaving behind an old identity.

I've realized that the endings of these chapters create clarity. Each of them was a moment to reflect, but also the end of one "life" and moving on to the next. A side effect of that is also the strong realization that time is not an unlimited commodity.

Perhaps the awareness of time is not meant to create fear, but clarity.

Rather than worrying about "How much time do I have left?", it is wiser to ask "Given the time I have left, what kind of person do I want to become?"

There are countless things we can do with the limited time we have, and that makes choosing what truly matters one of the most important decisions we make.

Maybe this is why major transitions feel so powerful. They remind us that we are allowed to let go of an old version of ourselves, reinvent, and begin again with intention.

It also provides a critical point of reflection to prioritize on the things that matter the most to us.

So maybe, living properly means choosing carefully how we spend our limited time, while never losing faith in our ability to grow.
